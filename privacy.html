Privacy Policy

App: ArrowVerse: Escape Puzzle

Developer: SSDEVLOPER

Last updated: November 18, 2026

SSDEVLOPER respects your privacy. This policy explains how ArrowVerse: Escape Puzzle handles game progress, optional online player features, advertising, and privacy choices.

1. Information We Process

ArrowVerse stores game-related information locally, including puzzle progress, coins, rewards, settings, and unlocked items.

Online features may use Firebase Anonymous Authentication, which assigns a unique anonymous user identifier. This does not require players to provide an email address or phone number.

2. Optional Private Player Reporting

If you explicitly enable private profile synchronization, the game may send your chosen nickname, anonymous player identifier, completed levels, stars, app version, and report timestamps to Firebase.

This information is used for player support and game activity reporting. Private player reports are intended to be accessible only to authorized studio administrators, not to other players.

This feature is optional and separate from League participation. You can disable future private reports through the game's Privacy & Data settings.

3. League and Leaderboard

Players who voluntarily join the League may have their nickname, score, stars, and completed-level progress displayed to other authenticated players on the leaderboard.

Equipped player avatars may also be displayed when supported by the current game version.

4. Offline Gameplay

ArrowVerse is designed to support offline puzzle gameplay. Local game progress can remain available without an internet connection. Online features and advertisements may require internet access.

5. Advertising

ArrowVerse is being prepared to use Google AdMob for advertising. When advertising is enabled in a released version, ads may include banners, interstitials, and optional rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process device, advertising, interaction, and technical information according to their policies and applicable consent requirements.

Advertising consent, where required, is separate from optional private player-profile synchronization. Appropriate privacy and consent choices will be provided when applicable.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy .

6. Rewards, Coins and Avatars

The game may include virtual coins, daily rewards, lucky spins, collectible avatars, and optional rewarded advertisements.

These virtual items are used within the game. Rewarded advertisements, when available, are optional.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards, including Firebase authentication and database access controls, to protect applicable player information.

However, no application, device, or internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game information generally remains on your device until it is deleted through applicable game or device controls.

Disabling private profile synchronization stops eligible future private reports but does not automatically delete information already stored in Firebase.

To request deletion of an existing private player report, contact SSDEVLOPER using the support email below. We may request reasonable information to verify control of the relevant game profile.

Public League information and other records may require a separate review or request.

9. Children's Privacy

ArrowVerse is designed for a broad audience. Advertising and data-handling configurations will be reviewed for applicable age-related and children's privacy requirements before public release.

Parents or guardians may contact us regarding privacy questions or deletion requests concerning a child.

10. Third-Party Services

Depending on the app version and enabled features, ArrowVerse may use:

  • Google Firebase Authentication
  • Google Cloud Firestore
  • Google AdMob (when enabled)

These services may process technical information required to operate their respective features.

11. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the game evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page, including an updated revision date.
