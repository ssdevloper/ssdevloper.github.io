This policy explains how SSDEVLOPER handles information for ArrowVerse: Escape Puzzle on Android. Contact us at shekharjha2018@gmail.com.

1. Intended audience and content rating

ArrowVerse is intended and marketed for adults aged 18 and above and is not directed to children. This describes our target audience. It is not the game's store content rating, which is determined separately under each store's rating system. It does not mean the app verifies your age. Existing known under-age advertising preferences are not silently changed to adult consent. A parent or guardian may contact us about a privacy concern.

2. Local gameplay

Levels, coins, avatars, settings and active puzzles are saved on your device. The game can be played offline. We do not upload a full game save, wallet or puzzle layout as part of profile reporting. Uninstalling or clearing device app data can erase local progress. A leaderboard profile is not a full-save backup.

3. Optional online League and Firebase guest identity

Joining the League is optional and uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication, which creates or reuses a persistent guest identifier. A technical guest identity is different from a player email/password or cross-device recovery account. When you join, your chosen nickname, guest UID, completed levels, stars, score and update timestamp may be stored in Firebase and shown to other League users. Only joined players appear in the competitive leaderboard. Do not use a nickname that reveals information you do not wish to make public.

4. Optional private reporting

Private profile reporting is a separate choice and is off by default for new players. The initial free release does not enable private-profile or custom-monitoring uploads. Where a future approved version enables reporting and you explicitly opt in, it may report nickname, guest UID, completed levels, stars, app version and report timestamps privately to authorised SSDEVLOPER administrators. It does not automatically join the League. Turning it off stops future eligible reports and clears unsent reports; an in-flight request may finish. Previously shared records require a deletion request. We do not silently enable optional reporting through Terms acceptance or advertising consent.

5. Advertising and Google privacy choices

The free release uses Google Mobile Ads: a Home banner, interstitials at selected natural level transitions, optional rewarded ads, and an App Open opportunity during eligible returning cold starts. App Open is limited locally to at most one opportunity per rolling 24 hours, skipped during initial setup and active puzzles. It does not silently download or install anything.

Rewarded ads are optional. Eligible completion and Daily Challenge bonuses, extra hearts, hints and Lucky Bonus Spin use the existing reward rules. A bonus is granted only after the SDK's earned-reward callback and successful local saving. Ad inventory or a reward opportunity is not guaranteed. Normal free gameplay and offline progress do not require personalised advertising.

Google and its providers may collect and share IP/network information (which may indicate general location), device or app-set identifiers as applicable, app/ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Removing Android advertising-ID permission does not prevent every other identifier or SDK data flow. Our private-reporting switch does not control Google's advertising processing.

We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to provide applicable advertising/privacy messages. Refusal, manage-options and privacy-choice controls are provided where required by Google's configured flow. Use Settings → Privacy & Data → Ad privacy choices to revisit available Google choices. If consent service or ads are unavailable, gameplay remains available. We do not treat an error as consent.

See Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

6. Purchases

Coin Packs and paid Remove Ads are not offered in this initial free ads-funded release. Earned in-game gold has no cash value and is not a payment account. Do not send payments to Support to unlock these features.

7. Support and deletion requests

Support may process information you deliberately send, such as your email address, nickname, existing player UID, app version and request details. Never send passwords, authentication tokens, signing keys or a full save.

You may request removal of studio-held online player data, including League, private reports and the Firebase guest identity as applicable. Use Settings → Privacy & Data → Request account/data deletion, or visit our deletion support instructions without opening the game. Preparing a request pauses studio sync locally but does not itself delete online records. Opening an email draft is not sending or completion confirmation. Support verifies ownership before acting; a UID or email alone is not sufficient proof. Local progress remains on the phone unless you separately remove it.

8. Retention and completion

Local data remains until removed by you or device/app controls. Studio online records remain while necessary for the service or until a verified deletion request is fulfilled, subject to narrowly justified security or legal retention. We explain required verification, retained data and expected completion when responding, and handle verified requests without undue delay under applicable law. Turning off sync is not deletion. Already-issued tokens or in-flight requests may delay complete removal; we recheck before confirming fulfilment. Rejoining or enabling sync afterwards may send new data. Google and other providers apply their own retention policies; contact information for their controls is separate from our studio records.

9. Security and processing locations

We use HTTPS for network requests and Android-protected storage for authentication secrets where supported. We do not claim the entire local save database is encrypted, or that any app is hack-proof. Data may be processed where Firebase, Google advertising or our support providers operate; we do not promise that every service processes all data in one country. We limit access and collection to the purposes described.

10. Changes and contact

We update this policy when relevant processing changes. New optional collection is not enabled merely by updating this page. For privacy questions or a deletion request, contact the Support address above or use the Support page.
