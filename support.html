Game Progress & Offline Play

ArrowVerse supports offline-first puzzle gameplay. If you experience issues with saved progress, coins, rewards or levels, contact support with your app version and a description of the problem.

Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or other sensitive credentials.

Privacy & Data Requests

You can review optional private player reporting in the game's Privacy & Data settings.

To request deletion of an existing private player report, email support with the subject "ArrowVerse Data Deletion Request".

We may ask you to verify control of the relevant game profile before processing your request.
