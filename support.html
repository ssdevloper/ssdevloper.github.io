Account and online player-data deletion requests

You can request deletion without opening or reinstalling the game. Email shekharjha2018@gmail.com with subject “ArrowVerse Data Deletion Request”.

  1. Identify ArrowVerse and tell us you want studio-held online player data removed. Include your existing UID if available and nickname/app version. If the app is unavailable or the UID is unknown, say so; we will explain how to identify the record securely.
  2. We verify control of the relevant identity before deletion. A public UID, nickname, email or screenshot alone is not enough. We never require passwords, authentication tokens or signing keys by email. Do not join the League or enable optional reporting merely to make a request.
  3. After verification we handle associated League listing, private reports, identifiable related monitoring records and Firebase guest identity, as applicable. We explain any legally/security-required retention and expected completion. Advertising-provider records have separate provider controls.
  4. We confirm completion after checking for outstanding/in-flight data. Local coins, levels, avatars and puzzles stay on your phone. Rejoining or turning sync on later can send new data.

In the game, Settings → Privacy & Data → Request account/data deletion prepares a request and pauses studio sync locally. You still need to send the email; a draft or copied text is not confirmation of receipt or deletion. No email app? Copy the request and send it from your preferred mail service when connected.

Requests are handled without undue delay under applicable law. Contact us if you need an update on verification or completion. The game has technical anonymous guest identities, not a player password/cross-device recovery feature; these instructions cover studio-held online player data regardless of the account-policy classification.

Read the Privacy Policy for data processing, advertising and retention. The intended audience is adults 18+; the store content rating is a separate classification.
